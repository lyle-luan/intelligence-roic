# Intelligence ROIC (I-ROIC)

**Intelligence ROIC** is a research project about a simple question:

> When intelligence becomes a scalable, purchasable and allocatable production factor, who captures the economic rent — and why?

The project studies AI not primarily as a model race, but as a capital-allocation problem. The core object is the path from energy and compute to intelligence, from intelligence to real-world outcomes, and from outcomes back into proprietary feedback loops.

A useful first-order chain is:

```text
Energy
  ↓
Compute
  ↓
Intelligence
  ↓
Intelligence Sink
  ↓
Economic / Physical Outcome
  ↓
Feedback
  ↺
```

The central working hypothesis is that model quality alone may become less scarce over time. Durable value may instead accrue to actors that can repeatedly direct intelligence into high-value, hard-to-copy sinks: proprietary data, workflows, installed bases, physical assets, distribution, memory, relationships, customer ownership, operating systems, or capital.

This repository is intentionally a **living research system**. It stores both:

1. **Current theory** — the best model we have today.
2. **Research process** — the reasoning path, discarded hypotheses, open questions and evidence that caused the model to change.

The second part is mandatory. I-ROIC should not become a collection of polished conclusions with no memory of how they were reached.

## Current research questions

- How should AI-native companies such as OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek and Kimi be valued if the highest-value use of their intelligence is internal production rather than token sales?
- How should cloud and internet companies be valued when they own models but mainly use them to improve existing businesses?
- How should open-source model ecosystems, compute factories and infrastructure providers be evaluated?
- What makes an **Intelligence Sink** high quality?
- Which companies can convert cheap intelligence into durable economic profit?
- How should Tesla, Xiaomi and other asset-rich operators be analyzed when AI can act through vehicles, devices, factories and other real-world endpoints?
- How does abundant intelligence alter firm boundaries, labor, capital intensity, margins and the distribution of economic surplus?
- What is the personal analogue of I-ROIC, and how should an individual accumulate memory, capital, distribution, relationships, owned businesses and agent organizations?

## Relationship to other projects

I-ROIC is intentionally separate from **Terminal Rent Cycle Strategy (TRC)**.

- **I-ROIC** asks: *What kinds of companies and assets can convert intelligence into durable economic rent?*
- **TRC** asks: *When, and at what price, should certain durable assets be purchased and later partially harvested through a cycle?*

The two can intersect in investment research, but they solve different problems.

I-ROIC also has a practical counterpart in AI organization systems. Company OS, persistent agents, personal knowledge systems and AI-native businesses can be treated as real-world experiments for measuring how intelligence turns into outcomes.
